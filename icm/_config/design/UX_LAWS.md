# UX laws

1. Within five seconds a first-time visitor understands who the site is for, what dos A does, and the primary next action.
2. Cinema supports comprehension; it never hides navigation, essential copy, quote actions, or accessibility.
3. No forced intro or “Entrar” gate.
4. Mobile is a separately art-directed first-class experience, not a desktop crop.
5. Essential content works with reduced motion and when cinematic media is unavailable.
6. Real project proof outranks decorative effects.
7. One primary CTA per decision moment.
8. Plain language beats internal, technical, or AI jargon.
