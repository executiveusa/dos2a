# Stage 05 — dos A cinematic scroll story

## Purpose
Create a scroll-driven cinematic journey that makes dos A feel precise, experienced, and capable at scale without delaying comprehension or making generated imagery look like false project proof.

## Non-negotiable narrative rule
The first screen is normal HTML and immediately states:

**Audio, video, iluminación y operación. Un solo equipo.**

The cinematic layer begins only after the visitor already understands who dos A is for and can reach `Cotizar mi evento` or `Hablar con La Genio`.

## Visual truth rule
AI-generated video is connective cinema, not evidence. Every scene must resolve into or sit beside verified real project photography. Generated footage must never be captioned as a completed client project. Real photos carry proof; generated motion carries continuity and atmosphere.

## Camera architecture
Use Scroll World **Architecture A: continuous forward take** for the grounded realistic journey.

- One model family for the complete chain.
- Each new leg starts from the actual final frame of the previous rendered leg.
- No camera-direction reversal across seams.
- Each leg may orbit, crane, track, or push inside the shot, but the final second settles into a slow forward drift so the next leg can continue naturally.
- Essential copy stays in HTML, never baked into generated frames.

## Copy timing
- Scene headline appears only after the camera establishes enough visual context.
- Keep one headline + one short support line visible at a time.
- Real proof label appears before/with the real photograph so visitors can distinguish proof from connective cinema.
- Avoid tag-pill clutter.

## Scroll behavior
- User can scroll normally through the page; the cinematic sequence must not trap the wheel/touch interaction.
- Use bounded sticky/pinned sections only where they aid comprehension.
- Copy, CTA, and navigation remain accessible while videos load.
- Reverse scroll must not expose visible seam pops.
