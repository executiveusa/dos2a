# Conversion contract

## First five seconds
A first-time visitor must see, without clicking or waiting for animation:

**dos A**

**Audio, video, iluminación y operación. Un solo equipo.**

`Producción técnica integral para empresas, agencias y organizadores de eventos en Ciudad de México y proyectos en todo México.`

Primary CTA: **Cotizar mi evento**

Secondary CTA: **Hablar con La Genio**

Trust line: **Más de 10 años de experiencia.**

The cinematic experience begins after orientation. No forced “Entrar”, no mystery splash screen, and no required animation before the user can act.

## Revenue circuit
`Visitante → consulta/brief → oportunidad calificada → seguimiento → llamada/visita técnica cuando aplique → cotización → aprobación → proyecto confirmado`

A conversion is successful only when the inquiry/event brief is durably persisted. Opening a mail client or attempting a notification is not a successful lead capture.

## CTA rules
- Each decision section has one primary action.
- `Cotizar mi evento` always starts or resumes the structured event brief.
- `Hablar con La Genio` starts the public concierge and can convert the conversation into the same structured event brief.
- Both paths converge on one durable opportunity model; never create duplicate shadow leads.
- If La Genio is unavailable, the normal quote form remains usable.
- If notification providers fail, the durable opportunity remains saved and notification retries happen separately.

## Public La Genio boundary
May:
- explain public services and process;
- ask event questions;
- accept text/voice input;
- structure an event brief;
- suggest relevant service categories;
- collect contact/consent;
- create a qualified opportunity;
- request a discovery call or quote.

May not:
- read private CRM/owner/project data;
- expose other customers;
- approve prices/contracts;
- invent availability;
- promise a booking;
- claim a final price from an ungoverned model response.

## Proof order
1. real project image/verified fact;
2. capability explanation;
3. process clarity;
4. CTA.

Avoid generic equipment lists above proof unless needed to answer a buyer question.
