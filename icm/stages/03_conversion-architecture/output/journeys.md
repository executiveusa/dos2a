# Buyer journeys

## 1 — Corporate event / marketing team
Need: confidence, coordination, brand-safe execution, one accountable team.

Path:
`Home → proof of integrated production → Eventos/Corporativo → case/portfolio proof → Cotizar → structured brief → follow-up/proposal`

Questions answered early:
- Can you coordinate audio, video, lighting, screens, staging/stands and operation together?
- Do you work in CDMX and elsewhere in Mexico?
- Can I bring you an incomplete idea and get help defining the technical scope?
- What happens after I submit?

## 2 — Agency / producer
Need: reliable technical partner that can fit inside a larger production, respond fast, and avoid operational surprises.

Path:
`Home → capabilities/proof → Servicios → Portafolio/Casos → Cotizar or La Genio → brief → technical follow-up`

The site must not imply dos A replaces the agency. Position dos A as an accountable technical/production partner that can coordinate its scope cleanly.

## 3 — Visitor who does not know what they need
Path:
`Home → Hablar con La Genio → simple questions by text/voice → event brief → recommended next step → contact captured → opportunity`

No technical questionnaire wall. Ask one necessary question at a time and explain why only when useful.

## 4 — Returning owner
Path:
`Authenticate → Inicio → three-to-five priorities → ask La Genio → prepare action → owner approval where required`

The owner sees plain language: `Tus oportunidades`, `Seguimiento`, `Cotizaciones`, `Proyectos`, not internal engineering/agent terminology.
