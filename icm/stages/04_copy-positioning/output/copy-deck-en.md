### One operation
**Everything has to work at the same time.**

Audio cannot run separately from screens while operations are improvised at the end. We coordinate the technical production as one system so each part arrives on time and does its job.

### Audio + video
**Make sure they hear it. Make sure they see it. Make sure the message lands.**

We plan sound, screens, projection, content, and signal around the room, audience, and event format.

### Lighting
**Atmosphere is designed, too.**

Lighting can organize a stage, transform a room, highlight a brand, and change how a moment feels. We treat it as part of the experience, not an add-on at the end.

### Stages, stands, and spaces
**From the stage to the whole space.**

We coordinate stages, structures, exhibition stands, scenic elements, screens, and brand elements so the physical environment and technology work together.

### Experiences
**We do not deliver isolated pieces. We build complete experiences.**

### Who it is for
**Companies** — Conferences, meetings, launches, conventions, and internal events where the message and the operation both matter.

**Agencies and producers** — A technical team that can fit into a larger production, respect the creative concept, and own its agreed scope without creating more work for the agency.

**Brands and activations** — Screens, lighting, stands, stages, and experiences that help a brand idea exist in a real space.

### Process
1. **Tell us about the event** — Date, venue, audience, objective, and what is already defined.
2. **We define the scope** — We identify what needs audio, video, lighting, staging, stands, or technical operation.
3. **We prepare the proposal** — We present the scope and refine it before confirmation.
4. **We produce and operate** — We coordinate setup, testing, operation, and teardown for the agreed scope.

## Services `/servicios`

**H1**
The technical side of your event, coordinated from start to finish.

Audio, video, lighting, stages, stands, and technical operation. We can handle one defined need or coordinate several disciplines as one team.

### Audio
Sound for speech, music, presentations, and hybrid formats, planned around coverage, intelligibility, audience size, and event flow.

### Video and screens
LED screens, projection, displays, signal, and content support for presentations, stages, stands, and brand experiences.

### Lighting
Stage, ambient, and architectural lighting planned around the space, camera, audience, and key moments.

### Stages, stands, and scenic environments
Stages, stands, structures, scenic elements, and brand components coordinated with screens, lighting, and operational requirements.

### Technical operation and coordination
We plan timing, setup, testing, operation, and teardown for our technical scope so responsibilities stay clear across disciplines.

### Special requirements
Depending on the project, the scope may include support for simultaneous interpretation, computers, effects, mapping, photography, recording, or other technical needs. These are confirmed case by case in the proposal.
