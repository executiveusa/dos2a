# Copy rules

- Mexican Spanish first; English is natural parity, not word-for-word translation.
- Use `dos A`, never DOS2A/D2A as public brand.
- Prefer buyer outcomes and responsibilities over inventory dumps.
- Do not use unsupported speed promises such as `respuesta en 24h` unless an operating SLA exists.
- Do not publish exact lead counts, ratings, customer counts, years above 10+, or client outcomes without evidence.
- Do not call a quote final until owner-approved.
- Do not call a booking confirmed until the governed booking state says confirmed.
- Avoid technical jargon in public copy and owner surfaces.
- Avoid generic AI phrases and Emerald Tablets banned hype words.
- Explain services in the language of what the event needs to accomplish.
- La Genio speaks like a practical Mexican business assistant: direct, calm, clear, and never pretends to have authority it does not have.
